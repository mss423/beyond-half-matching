# Beyond the Half-Approximation

**Fair and Efficient Online Class Matching**  
Sander Borst · Max Springer

[**Explore the interactive companion →**](https://mss423.github.io/beyond-half-matching/neurips-2026/) · [**Read the paper (PDF) →**](https://mss423.github.io/beyond-half-matching/neurips-2026/neurips_2026.pdf)

This companion lets you read the paper through its theorem statements, proofs, and supporting results. Start with a main theorem, follow its dependencies, and inspect the paper's text at each step.

## Explore the proofs

- **Choose a result** on the graph or search for a theorem, lemma, or source label.
- **Follow the argument:** “Uses” lists supporting results; “Used by” shows results that depend on the selected one. An arrow points from a supporting result to the result it helps prove.
- **Read the details:** each result includes its statement, available proof passages, and expandable LaTeX source. Click mathematical notation to open its definition.
- **Focus your view:** use Main, All, or Focus to navigate the graph, or switch to Read only for a larger reading pane.
- **Return to the paper:** “Open in paper” links to the result's page in the PDF.

Graph positions and distances are schematic. Numbered proof steps are navigation through the paper's proof text.
